About LAOTESI.

Kitchenware expertise.
A human commitment.

We make stainless steel kitchenware for everyday life — and help brands make it their own. Our story began in 1985 in Caitang, Chaozhou, one of China’s established stainless steel manufacturing centers.

Let’s build something together

From a family workshop.
To your manufacturing partner.

Chaozhou Laotesi Enterprise Co., Ltd. brings together development, design, manufacturing and international trade. Over decades of growth, a family workshop has become a standardized, large-scale kitchenware business, with products exported to more than 100 countries and regions, including the United States, Germany, Japan and South Korea.

Our range includes stainless steel food storage containers, cookware, kettles, basins, bowls and cups. By connecting practical design with material and manufacturing expertise, we create products that make daily cooking, serving and storage easier.
