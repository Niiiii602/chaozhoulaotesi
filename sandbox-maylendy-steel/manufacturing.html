01 / Material control

Identify the steel before production.

Stainless sheets and coils are identified, staged and checked before they enter the line. Material grade, thickness and surface are reviewed before forming.

Incoming material · Grade / thicknessDiscuss this checkpoint
02 / Sheet preparation & forming

Turn released stock into product bodies.

Shearing and press forming turn released stainless stock into consistent blanks and bodies. The forming stations define each product shape.

Shearing · Deep drawingDiscuss this checkpoint
03 / Tooling & changeovers

Make repeat setups more consistent.

Dedicated dies and forming tools are kept visible and ready for the next approved setup. Organized tooling storage supports changeovers and repeatable OEM production.

Dies & fixtures · Repeatable setupDiscuss this checkpoint
04 / Quality checks & testing

Check the details that matter in use.

Routine checks confirm dimensions, finish and product performance before pack-out. The inspection area supports dimensional, weight, leakage and freezer-performance checks.

Dimensions · Performance checksDiscuss this checkpoint
05 / Packing & shipment staging

Protect the product through pack-out.

Protected parts are held between operations and prepared for private-label packing and export loading. Identified staging areas keep finished and semi-finished parts organized.
