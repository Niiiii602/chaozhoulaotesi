Materials & construction

What materials are available for the container body?

Food-storage bodies can be specified in 304 stainless steel, 316L stainless steel or titanium. Select the material with the model and destination market; matching lids and silicone seals are confirmed as part of the product specification.

Link to this answer
What capacities and shapes can I choose from?

The catalog includes rectangular, square, round and divided containers, from 280 ml portions to selected 6,600 ml models, plus coordinated sets. Stated capacities are brimful capacities, and external dimensions include the lid. The nominal food-storage body thickness is 0.4 mm. The 01RC2000 reference is 2,000 ml / 68 oz.

Additional 04-series containers with stainless steel or bamboo lids can also be discussed. Review the specific model dimensions before ordering.

Link to this answer
Which lid options are available?

The core catalog includes integrated PP lids, PP lids with detachable clips, silicone-wrapped glass lids and pure silicone lids. High-clarity MS lids are available for 01RC bodies. Stainless steel and bamboo lid options are also available in the 04 series. Sealing rings are silicone; match the body, lid and seal before sample approval.

Link to this answer
Are the containers leak-proof?

The core food-storage range uses an airtight, leak-resistant closure design. Review sealing performance for the selected body, lid and silicone gasket, especially when choosing a different lid family. Keep the sealing surfaces clean, fit the gasket correctly and close the lid securely.

Do divided containers keep different foods separate?

Our two-compartment containers come in standard and fully partitioned versions. The fully partitioned version helps keep foods apart and limits mixing to a degree; the standard version does not isolate the compartments.

Three-compartment containers currently come in the standard version only. We can develop a different structure to meet your requirements, and a fully partitioned four-compartment version is currently in development.

Everyday use & care

Can the containers go in the microwave?

Microwave compatibility must be confirmed for the specific container model and appliance instructions. For a model approved for microwave reheating, remove the lid. Do not assume that stainless steel or titanium material alone makes a container microwave-compatible.

Ask for the applicable model instructions and testing scope before defining consumer-use claims or heating settings.

Link to this answer
Can the containers be used in an oven?

For a stainless steel body approved for oven use, remove the lid and follow the specified temperature limit. The existing stainless-body reference is −20 to 230°C; this does not give the complete container or every lid the same rating.

Open-flame and induction suitability require separate model-specific confirmation. Select a cookware model for stovetop cooking unless the chosen food-storage body is explicitly approved for that use. Removing a lid alone does not establish compatibility.

Link to this answer
What temperatures can the container and lid withstand?

The temperature ranges for the individual materials are:

Stainless steel body
−20 to 230°C
PP lid
−20 to 90°C
Silicone lid
−40 to 180°C

These are component limits, not a single rating for the assembled container. Remove the lid for approved oven or microwave use. The reference ranges do not establish suitability for flame or induction cooking. Please ask us for the limits of MS lids or other lid assemblies.

Link to this answer
Are they suitable for refrigerator and freezer storage?

Yes. Our food storage containers can be used in both refrigerators and freezers. Leave space for food and liquids to expand when freezing, and follow the care instructions for the selected model.

Link to this answer
Are the products dishwasher-safe?

The core food-storage range supports dishwasher cleaning according to the selected model and lid instructions. Remove and clean detachable seals as directed, and allow components to dry thoroughly before reassembly. Confirm separate care instructions for bamboo lids; do not apply the core plastic/silicone-lid guidance automatically to wood components.

Link to this answer
How should consumers clean and care for stainless steel?

Wash before first use with mild detergent and a soft sponge, then rinse and dry. Avoid abrasive steel wool and chlorine-based cleaners. Clean removable seals separately according to the care instructions, and do not leave salty or acidic food residue on the steel for extended periods.

How durable are stainless steel food storage containers?

Stainless steel bodies are intended for repeated everyday use and do not shatter like glass. They can still scratch or dent, and seals and closures can wear over time. Evaluate the sample for the handling, wash cycles and transport conditions expected in your market.

Customization & ordering

How can we customize logos and colors?

The stainless steel body can carry a laser-engraved logo or a color screen-printed logo. Lids can be laser-engraved, screen-printed or decorated with a color heat-transfer logo.

Both lid colors and silicone gasket colors can be customized. Contact us with your ideas, and we will provide a professional branding and color solution for your range.

Can you develop a new size, shape or lid design?

Custom sizes, shapes, handles, lids and sealing solutions can start from a drawing, idea or physical reference. Most molds take 30–40 calendar days from receipt of the tooling deposit, with a 30-day target for suitable projects.

The period includes mold trials and revisions. Technical feasibility and unresolved development issues can affect the schedule and will be discussed with you.

What is the minimum order quantity?

The standard MOQ is 1,000 pieces per product model. Mixed-model shipments are supported; the MOQ still applies to each model. Smaller orders may be accepted with a small-order charge, subject to confirmation.

Packaging and printing also have a standard MOQ of 1,000; smaller quantities incur additional charges. Quotations are valid for 30 days.

Can I review samples and approve the final order details?

Stock and customized samples can be arranged in quantities of up to 10 pieces. Samples are charged at the product unit price plus shipping; tooling and printing are charged at actual cost. The buyer pays freight and taxes.

Sample product fees are credited against a subsequent order. Regular sampling usually takes 3–10 days after payment. Review finish, lid fit, seal and handling before approving production.

What retail and export packaging options can you provide?

Packaging can be discussed around the product, pack quantity and sales channel. Available options include blister packs, PP bags, printed color boxes, kraft boxes, display boxes and supermarket display stands.

Confirm the artwork, protection and packing details for the quotation. The standard packaging/printing MOQ is 1,000, with small-order charges below that quantity.

What is the lead time for a wholesale order?

Regular production usually takes 40–45 calendar days from receipt of deposit to production completion. Peak-season planning is typically 40–50 calendar days. Shipping time is not included.

Suitable single-model orders of 3,000–5,000 pieces can start from 30 calendar days. The selected model, customization and confirmed schedule determine the final lead time; this is not a blanket 30-day delivery promise.

Can we arrange an inspection before shipment?

Customers can arrange third-party or pre-shipment inspection to their requirements. Standard sampling is based on ISO 2859-1, General Inspection Level II, normal single sampling, with AQL 2.5 for major defects and AQL 4.0 for minor defects.

Critical defects are not accepted. The critical-defect sampling and acceptance rules, lot size and any customer-specific requirements are agreed in the inspection plan. Appearance and functional performance are checked, alongside the agreed shipment requirements.

What payment methods and shipment ports are available?

Payment can be arranged by T/T or L/C, in USD or RMB. The standard schedule is a 30% deposit to confirm the order and start production scheduling, followed by the 70% balance after inspection and before shipment.

Shipment ports include Shantou, Shenzhen and Xiamen. Trade terms, payment details and special requirements are agreed for the individual order. Quotations remain valid for 30 days.

How is monthly factory capacity defined?

Normal output is approximately 600,000 pieces per month, with peak output of approximately 800,000. The 600,000-piece figure is the normal planning basis for the combined stainless kitchenware, food-storage and cookware production, based on operating records since 2020.

These totals are not the capacity of each individual model. Confirm your product mix and delivery schedule with the team.
